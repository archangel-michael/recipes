## Ingredients

- 6 oz (170 g) chicken breast
- 1 cup (160 g) cooked jasmine rice
- 1/2 cup (75 g) diced red bell pepper
- 1/2 cup (75 g) diced yellow onion
- 1/3 cup (85 g) light cream cheese
- 1/4 cup (60 ml) unsweetened almond milk
- 1 tbsp (8 g) Cajun seasoning
- 2 cloves (6 g) garlic
- 1 tsp (5 ml) olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste

---

## Directions

1. Season the chicken with the Cajun seasoning, plus salt and pepper to taste.
2. Heat the olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the chicken 4-5 minutes per side, until cooked through (165°F / 74°C inside). Remove and slice.
3. In the same skillet, add the onion and bell pepper. Saute 3-4 minutes until softened.
4. Lower the heat and stir in the cream cheese and almond milk until smooth and creamy.
5. Add the cooked rice and sliced chicken back to the skillet. Stir to combine, then serve.
