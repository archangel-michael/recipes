## Ingredients

- 6 oz (170 g) lean ground beef (93/7)
- 7 oz (200 g) potatoes, russet or yellow
- 1 oz (28 g) reduced-fat cheddar cheese, shredded
- 1 cup (50 g) romaine lettuce, chopped
- 1/2 cup (75 g) cherry tomatoes, halved
- 2 tbsp (30 g) pickles, chopped or sliced
- 2 tbsp (20 g) red onion, diced
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) lighter burger sauce
- Olive oil, for tossing the potatoes
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- Garlic powder, for the beef

---

## Directions

1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Dice the potatoes, toss with olive oil, salt and pepper, and roast 20 to 25 minutes, until crispy.
2. Cook the ground beef in a skillet over medium heat. Season with salt, pepper and garlic powder. Drain excess fat.
3. In a bowl, add the roasted potatoes and seasoned beef.
4. Top with the cheddar, lettuce, tomatoes, pickles, red onion and burger sauce.
5. Mix and enjoy.
