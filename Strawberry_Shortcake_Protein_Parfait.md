# Strawberry Shortcake Protein Parfait

> A no-cook layered parfait of vanilla whey-sweetened Greek yogurt, strawberries and crushed shortcake or graham crackers. About 34 g protein per glass (post), likely closer to 40+ g.

## Ingredients

- 3/4 cup (170 g) Greek yogurt, 0-2% fat
- 1 scoop (30 g) vanilla whey protein
- 1 cup (150 g) strawberries, fresh or frozen
- 2 tbsp (14 g) shortcake cookies or graham crackers, crushed, low-fat
- 1 tsp (5 ml) sugar-free maple syrup (or honey)
- 1/2 tsp (2 ml) vanilla extract
- 1/4 tsp (0.5 g) cinnamon (optional)

---

## Directions

1. In a bowl, mix the Greek yogurt, whey protein, maple syrup (or honey) and vanilla extract until smooth and creamy.
2. Layer in a glass or jar: protein cream, strawberries, and crushed shortcake.
3. Repeat the layers, then top with the remaining strawberries and a sprinkle of crumbs.
4. Enjoy.

---

## Notes

- The post says "1/4 cup (170 g)" of yogurt. 170 g is about 3/4 cup, so the cup measure looks like a typo; the gram weight is used here.
- Servings, prep time and the "no bake" label are inferred: it is one glass, matching the single set of macros in the post.
- If using frozen strawberries, thaw and drain them first so the layers do not turn watery.
- Pro tip from the post: use low-fat shortcake cookies or graham crackers to keep it lighter while keeping the classic flavor.
- The cinnamon is optional; the post shows it in the ingredient list but not in the method.
- Macros depend on your protein powder and yogurt. Honey instead of sugar-free syrup adds about 20 cal and 6 g carbs.
