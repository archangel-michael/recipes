## Ingredients

**Cake**
- 1 1/4 cups (300 g) low-fat cottage cheese
- 2 large eggs
- 2/3 cup (150 g) egg whites (about 5)
- About 3 medium carrots, grated (200 g)
- 1 cup (100 g) oat flour
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 1/2 to 3 tsp cinnamon
- 1/2 tsp ground ginger
- 1/4 tsp nutmeg
- 1/4 tsp salt
- Sweetener to taste

**Cream Cheese Frosting**
- 2/3 cup (150 g) light cream cheese
- 3/4 cup (200 g) 0% Greek yogurt
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- Sweetener to taste
- Pinch of salt

---

## Directions

1. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease an 8x8-inch baking dish.
2. Blend all the cake ingredients except the carrot until smooth.
3. Fold in the grated carrot and pour into the dish.
4. Bake 30 to 35 minutes, until the center is set.
5. Cool completely.
6. Mix the frosting ingredients until smooth, spread over the cake, and cut into 9 squares.
7. Store in the fridge up to 4 days.

## Notes

- The post did not give a pan size; 8x8 is inferred for 9 squares. Check at 30 minutes, since a smaller pan may need longer.
- Don't hold back on the sweetener; the post says the recipe needs it.
- Cool the cake fully before frosting or the frosting will melt.
- Macros depend on the fat level of your cottage cheese and cream cheese.
