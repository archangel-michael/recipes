## Ingredients

**Pancakes**
- 1 scoop (30 g) vanilla whey protein
- 1/2 cup (40 g) oat flour
- 1/2 cup (113 g) low-fat cottage cheese
- 1 large egg (50 g)
- 1/4 cup (60 ml) unsweetened almond milk
- 1 1/2 tsp (4 g) cinnamon
- 1 tsp (4 g) baking powder
- Cooking spray

**Topping**
- 2 tbsp (30 g) sugar-free icing (optional)
- Extra cinnamon for sprinkling

---

## Directions

1. Add the protein, oat flour, cottage cheese, egg, almond milk, cinnamon and baking powder to a blender.
2. Blend until smooth and thick, about 20-30 seconds.
3. Heat a nonstick skillet over medium heat and lightly coat with cooking spray.
4. Pour the batter into the pan as pancakes and cook 2-3 minutes per side, until golden and cooked through.
5. Drizzle with the sugar-free icing (optional), add a sprinkle of cinnamon, and enjoy.
