# Chorizo Breakfast Burritos

**Saved:** 2026-09-25

|----------|----------|---------|-------|-----|
| 10       | 450      | 50g     | 18g   | 20g |

## Ingredients

### Burritos
- 10 chorizo chicken sausages (840g)
- 20 whole eggs (~1000g)
- 1 Tbsp (15g) garlic salt
- 0.5 Tbsp (2g) black pepper
- 1.5 cups (270g) fat free cheddar, shredded
- 2.5 cups (450g) fat free mozzarella, shredded
- 10 large low calorie tortillas

### Chipotle Mayo
- 0.5 cup (120g) light mayo
- 2 Tbsp (30g) minced chipotle peppers
- 1 lime, juiced
- 0.25 Tbsp (3g) garlic salt
- 0.25 Tbsp (1g) smoked paprika

---

## Instructions

### 1. Cook the chorizo
Remove sausages from casings if needed and crumble into a large skillet. Cook over medium heat until browned and fully cooked, 8-10 minutes.

### 2. Scramble the eggs
Whisk the eggs with garlic salt and black pepper. Scramble in a separate pan (or the same skillet once the chorizo is done) until just set — don't overcook, they'll finish warming when assembled.

### 3. Combine the filling
Fold the cooked chorizo and scrambled eggs together with the fat free cheddar and mozzarella until the cheese is evenly distributed and starting to melt from the residual heat.

### 4. Make the chipotle mayo
Whisk together light mayo, minced chipotle peppers, lime juice, garlic salt, and smoked paprika until smooth. Refrigerate until needed.

### 5. Assemble the burritos
Warm the tortillas slightly so they're pliable. Divide the filling evenly among the 10 tortillas (~260g filling each). Add a drizzle of chipotle mayo if eating fresh, fold in the sides, and roll tightly.

### 6. Serve or freeze
Eat immediately, or cool completely before freezing (see below). Save the rest of the chipotle mayo as a dip/side.

---

## Freezing Instructions

1. Wrap each burrito in aluminum foil, or place in an airtight freezer bag.
2. Freeze for up to 3 months.

## Reheating Instructions

1. Loosely wrap a frozen burrito in a wet paper towel.
2. Microwave on a plate for 5-6 minutes.
3. Toast in a pan or air fry for a few minutes if you want it crispy.

---

## Notes

- Cook instructions were inferred from the ingredient list — the original post only gave macros, ingredients, and freezing/reheating steps.
- Fat free cheeses melt differently than full-fat versions — low-medium heat helps avoid a rubbery texture.
- 50g protein per burrito is high-value for a breakfast option — a good whole-food swap-in for the shake/bar-based breakfasts already in rotation.
