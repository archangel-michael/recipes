## Ingredients

- 6 oz (170 g) chicken breast
- 1 cup (160 g) cooked jasmine rice
- 1 1/2 cups (150 g) broccoli florets
- 1 tbsp (21 g) honey
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) low-sodium soy sauce
- 2 cloves (6 g) garlic, minced
- 1 tsp (5 ml) olive oil
- 1 tbsp (5 g) green onion, sliced, for garnish
- 1/2 tsp (2 g) sesame seeds
- Salt and pepper

---

## Directions

1. Season the chicken with salt and pepper. Cook in a pan with the olive oil for 4 to 5 minutes per side, until cooked through. Let rest, then slice.
2. In the same pan, add the garlic, soy sauce and honey. Simmer 1 to 2 minutes until slightly thickened.
3. Cook the rice according to the package directions.
4. Steam or saute the broccoli until crisp-tender.
5. Assemble the bowl with rice, chicken, broccoli and extra sauce. Top with green onion and sesame seeds.
