# Two-Minute Fudgy Protein Brownie

> A single-serve microwave brownie built on egg, protein yogurt, cocoa and protein powder. Fast, low fat, and very high protein for the calories.

## Ingredients

- 1 large egg
- 1/3 cup + 1 tsp (100 g, 3.5 oz) high-protein Greek yogurt
- About 4 tbsp (20 g) unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1 tbsp + 1 tsp (20 g) sugar-free maple syrup
- 1 scoop (30 g, 1 oz) chocolate whey/casein blend protein powder

---

## Directions

1. Add everything to a microwave-safe bowl or ramekin and mix until smooth.
2. Microwave 90 seconds to 2 minutes, depending on your microwave.
3. Optional toppings: vanilla yogurt, low-calorie ice cream, or crushed nuts.

---

## Notes

- For a gooey center, stop early, but the egg should be set, not liquid.
- Whey/casein can dry out in the microwave, so do not overcook.
- Sugar-free maple syrup brands vary; check the carbs on yours.
