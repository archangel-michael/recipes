# Chocolate Hazelnut Protein Spread

> A Nutella-style spread made from roasted hazelnuts, casein-type chocolate protein powder and cocoa. About 9 g protein per 2 tbsp.

## Ingredients

- 6 tbsp + 1 tsp (95 mL) unsweetened almond milk
- 1/4 cup (34 g) raw hazelnuts
- 2 scoops (about 60 g) chocolate protein powder, casein or whey/casein blend
- 1 1/2 tbsp (8 g) unsweetened cocoa powder
- 2 1/2 tbsp (30 g) granulated sugar substitute
- 1/4 tsp salt
- Optional, not in the macros: 1/3 tsp (1 g) xanthan gum

---

## Directions

1. Roast the hazelnuts at 350°F for 8-10 minutes, then rub off the skins in a towel.
2. Blend the hazelnuts alone until smooth, glossy and runny.
3. Add everything else and blend until thick and smooth.
4. Too thick: add almond milk 1 tsp at a time. Too thin: rest it, then add a pinch of xanthan gum.
5. Chill 30-45 minutes (it firms up overnight). Stir before using.

---

## Notes

- Casein or a whey/casein blend gives the thick texture; pure whey will be runnier.
- Keep refrigerated and use within about a week.
