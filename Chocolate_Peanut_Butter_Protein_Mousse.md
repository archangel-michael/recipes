## Ingredients

- 1 cup (240 g) Greek yogurt, 0-2% fat
- 1 scoop (30 g) chocolate whey protein
- 1 tbsp (16 g) natural peanut butter
- 1 tbsp (5 g) unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) maple syrup (or sugar-free syrup)
- 1/2 tsp (2 ml) vanilla extract

**Topping**
- 1 tbsp (10 g) dark chocolate (70%+), chopped
- 1 tbsp (7 g) crushed peanuts (optional)

---

## Directions

1. Add the Greek yogurt, whey protein, peanut butter, cocoa powder, maple syrup and vanilla extract to a bowl.
2. Whisk until smooth, thick and creamy.
3. Top with the chopped dark chocolate and crushed peanuts.
4. Refrigerate 5 to 10 minutes (optional) for a thicker, mousse-like texture.
5. Enjoy.
