# Butter Bean Blondies (Lighter, High-Protein Tweak)

> Fudgy blondies blended from butter beans and oats. This is the lighter version: half the sugar swapped for allulose, dark chips instead of white, and a scoop of whey. **Untested tweak: the original is reliable, the changes below are my adjustments.**

## Ingredients

- 1 can (15 oz) butter beans, drained and rinsed (about 1.5 cups, 252 g)
- 1/3 cup light brown sugar (about 75 g) *(original: 3/4 cup, 150 g)*
- 1/3 cup allulose (about 75 g) *(new)*
- 2 large eggs
- 2 tsp vanilla extract (8 g)
- 2/3 cup rolled oats (60 g)
- 1 scoop (about 30 g) vanilla whey protein *(new)*
- 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp baking soda
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/4 cup unsalted butter, melted (55 g)
- 1/4 cup dark chocolate chips *(original: 1/2 cup white chips, plus 2 tbsp as topping)*

---

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Lightly grease or line an 8x8 pan.
2. In a high-speed blender, blend the butter beans, both sugars, eggs, vanilla, oats, whey, baking powder, baking soda and salt until completely smooth, 2-3 minutes. Add the melted butter and blend again until smooth.
3. Transfer the batter to a bowl and fold in the dark chips.
4. Pour into the pan and smooth the top. Bake 30-35 minutes, until a toothpick comes out mostly clean with a few moist crumbs. Allulose browns faster than sugar, so check at 30 minutes.
5. Cool completely in the pan, about 30 minutes, then cut into 9 squares.
6. Store covered in the fridge up to 5 days.

---

## Notes

- **Untested:** whey in baking can dry the texture. If the batter is very thick, blend in 1-2 tbsp milk. If it comes out dry, drop the whey next time.
- To go back to the original, use 3/4 cup brown sugar, no allulose or whey, and 1/2 cup white chips (plus 2 tbsp sprinkled on top 10-15 minutes into baking).
