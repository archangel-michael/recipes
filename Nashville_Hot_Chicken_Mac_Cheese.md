## Ingredients

### Crispy Nashville Hot Chicken
- 2187 g boneless skinless chicken breast, cubed
- 1 Tbsp black pepper
- 5 tsp smoked paprika
- 4 tsp garlic powder
- 4 tsp onion powder
- 3 tsp chili powder
- 4 Tbsp baking powder
- 4 Tbsp dark brown sugar
- 4 tsp olive oil
- 60 g reduced-fat butter (for cooking), plus extra for finishing
- Finishing dry rub, to taste: smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder
- 4 Tbsp fresh parsley, chopped

### Creamy Mac n Cheese
- 24 oz Brami protein pasta, dry
- 880 g 1% milk
- 500 g 50% fat cream cheese
- 250 g low-fat sharp cheddar
- 4 Tbsp mustard
- 2.5 tsp each: smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder
- 1.25 tsp each: black pepper, chili powder
- 4 Tbsp fresh parsley, chopped

---

## Directions

1. **Cook the chicken** — Cook in light butter over medium heat, 4-5 minutes per side, in batches, until a crispy golden-brown crust forms. Pull at 165°F; breast dries out fast.
2. **Coat** — Lower the heat, add extra butter, the dry spice mix and parsley. Toss until well coated and the spices are rubbed in.
3. **Make the sauce** — In a pan on medium-low, combine milk, cream cheese, cheddar, mustard, spices and parsley. Stir continuously until melted and smooth. Let it thicken slightly.
4. **Combine** — Cook the pasta, fold it into the sauce until coated and creamy, and serve topped with the chicken.

## Notes

- Original post made 5 servings with chicken thighs and macaroni; this version is doubled to 10.
- Chicken breast swapped for thighs; pull it as soon as it hits 165°F so it stays juicy.
- Brami pasta per 2 oz dry: 200 cal, 12g protein, 36g carbs, 2g fat, 5g fiber (24 oz = 12 servings).
- Sauce scaled up about 26% to match the extra pasta.
