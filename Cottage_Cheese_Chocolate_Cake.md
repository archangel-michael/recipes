# Cottage Cheese Chocolate Cake

> A five-ingredient baked chocolate cake made from cottage cheese and eggs. No protein powder, about 40 g protein.

---

## Ingredients

- 7/8 cup (200 g) cottage cheese
- 2 large eggs
- 4 tbsp powdered monk fruit or stevia
- 3 tbsp (15 g) unsweetened cacao or cocoa powder
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- Optional: sugar-free chocolate chips

---

## Directions

1. Heat the oven to 350°F (180°C). Grease a small 2-cup baking dish or two ramekins.
2. Blend or whisk everything except the chips until smooth. A blender gives the smoothest texture.
3. Pour into the dish, top with chips if using, and bake about 30 minutes, until just set at the edges.
4. Cool slightly and serve.

---

## Notes

- The pan size and the mixing step are inferred; the post gave neither.
- The post calls it a lava cake, but cottage cheese and eggs baked 30 minutes will set like a soft custard. Pull it a few minutes early for a softer center.
- Macros depend on the fat level of your cottage cheese.
