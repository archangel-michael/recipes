## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup (40 g) rolled oats
- 3/4 cup (170 g) Greek yogurt, 0-2% fat
- 1 scoop (30 g) vanilla whey protein
- 3/4 cup (100 g) blueberries, fresh or frozen
- 1 tbsp (15 g) light cream cheese
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) maple syrup, sugar-free or regular
- 1/2 tsp (2 ml) vanilla extract
- 1/2 tsp lemon zest (optional)
- 1/4 tsp cinnamon (optional)

---

## Directions

1. Add the oats, Greek yogurt, whey protein, cream cheese, maple syrup and vanilla extract to a bowl.
2. Stir until smooth and creamy.
3. Fold in the blueberries and lemon zest.
4. Let sit 3 to 5 minutes to thicken, or refrigerate overnight for a thicker texture.
5. Top with a few extra blueberries and a sprinkle of cinnamon if desired.
