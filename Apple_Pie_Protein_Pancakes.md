## Ingredients

### Pancakes
- 1 box (565g) Kodiak Power Cakes mix
- 1 egg (50g)
- 2.5 cups (600g) fat free milk
- 0.5 Tbsp (2g) cinnamon
- 1 can (565g) no sugar apple pie filling
- (Optional) 1 cup (100g) protein granola, for topping

### Icing (optional)
- 1 cup (128g) zero calorie confectioner sugar
- 2 Tbsp (30g) milk

---

## Instructions

### 1. Mix the batter
Whisk the Kodiak Power Cakes mix, egg, milk, and cinnamon together until just combined.

### 2. Fold in the apple pie filling
Gently fold in the no-sugar apple pie filling, keeping the apple chunks mostly intact.

### 3. Cook the pancakes
Heat a lightly greased griddle or non-stick skillet over medium heat. Pour batter in ~1/3-cup portions (makes about 10 pancakes). Cook 2-3 minutes per side, until bubbles form on the surface and the edges set, then flip and cook until golden.

### 4. Make the icing (optional)
Whisk the zero-calorie confectioner sugar and milk together until smooth. Drizzle over warm pancakes and top with protein granola if using.

---

## Freezing Instructions

1. Place the pancakes in meal prep containers or an airtight freezer bag.
2. Freeze for up to 3 months.

---

## Reheating Instructions

1. Take a frozen pancake and wrap it in a wet paper towel.
2. Microwave on a plate for 1-2 minutes.
3. Toast in a pan or air fry for a few minutes if you like it crispy.
