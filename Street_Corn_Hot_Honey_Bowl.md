# Street Corn Hot Honey Bowl

> Taco-seasoned beef over roasted sweet potato, topped with a creamy street corn salsa. Big bowl, high protein, easy meal prep. This is the higher-protein, lower-fat version (4 servings).

## Ingredients

**Beef**
- 2 lb (32 oz) 96% lean ground beef
- Salt and pepper
- 2-3 tbsp taco seasoning
- 1/4 cup water
- Avocado oil spray

**Sweet potatoes**
- 2 medium sweet potatoes, cubed
- Avocado oil spray
- Salt and pepper
- 1 tsp cornstarch

**Street corn salsa**
- 2 cups corn
- 1 cup low-fat cottage cheese, blended
- 1/4 cup nonfat plain Greek yogurt (replaces the 2 tbsp light mayo in the original)
- 3 tbsp cotija cheese (about 30 g)
- Juice and zest of 1 lime
- 1-2 jalapeños, finely diced
- 2-3 tbsp chopped cilantro
- 1 avocado, cubed
- 1 tsp Tajín (optional)

**Optional:** drizzle of hot honey. The original's name mentions it but the post lists no ingredient for it. About 1 tbsp adds about 60 cal and 16 g carbs per bowl.

---

## Directions

*The original post gave no directions; these are inferred.*

1. **Roast the sweet potatoes:** toss with oil spray, salt, pepper and cornstarch. Roast at 425°F for 25-30 minutes, turning once.
2. **Cook the beef:** brown in a skillet sprayed with oil, seasoned with salt, pepper and taco seasoning. Add 1/4 cup water and cook until it evaporates.
3. **Make the salsa:** blend the cottage cheese with the Greek yogurt, then stir in the corn, cotija, lime juice and zest, jalapeño, cilantro, avocado and Tajín.
4. **Build the bowls:** divide the sweet potatoes and beef into 4 bowls and top with the salsa. Drizzle with hot honey if using.
5. **Store:** keep the salsa separate from the hot beef and potatoes. Refrigerate up to 4 days.

---

## Notes

- The 96% lean beef is what keeps fat near 16 g per bowl. Using 93% adds about 8 g fat per bowl.
- To raise protein further, top each bowl with 3 oz grilled chicken (+26 g protein, +140 cal) or 3 scrambled egg whites (+11 g protein, +50 cal).
- Sodium runs high from the taco seasoning, cotija and Tajín. Use a low-sodium taco seasoning if that matters.
