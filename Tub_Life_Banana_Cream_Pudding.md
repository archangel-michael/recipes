# Tub Life Banana Cream Pudding

> A no-bake protein banana pudding mixed in one bowl and layered back into the yogurt tub with vanilla wafers. Fridge overnight, 5 servings.

Whole tub per the post: 1,145 cal / 144P / 121C / 13F. The post's per-serving numbers are "eyeballed". My own check from typical label values came to about 1,200 cal / 135P / 140C / 10F for a 32 oz tub, so carbs may run 24-28 g per serving. Check your brands.

---

## Ingredients

- 1 tub (32 oz / 907 g) nonfat vanilla Greek yogurt
- 16 vanilla wafers (2 servings, about 62 g)
- 1 1/2 scoops (about 45 g) vanilla whey
- 3/4 of a 1 oz box (about 21 g, roughly 3 tbsp) sugar-free banana cream pudding mix
- 1 medium banana (about 118 g), sliced
- 2 tbsp (12 g) peanut butter powder
- 6 tbsp (about 1/3 cup) fat-free whipped topping

---

## Directions

1. Combine everything except the wafers in a mixing bowl and mix well.
2. Layer it back into the yogurt tub with 2 layers of vanilla wafers.
3. Refrigerate overnight.
