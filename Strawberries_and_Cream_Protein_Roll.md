# Strawberries & Cream Protein Roll

> A thin cottage cheese and egg sponge, rolled around protein yogurt, light cream cheese and fresh berries. Very high protein for the calories, and a good post-lift snack.

## Ingredients

**Base**
- 1 cup (250 g) low-fat cottage cheese
- 2 large eggs
- 2 tbsp + 2 tsp cornstarch (20 g)
- 1 scoop (30 g) vanilla protein powder

**Filling**
- 3.5 tbsp (50 g) light mascarpone or light cream cheese
- 1 cup (250 g) vanilla protein yogurt (YoPRO or similar)
- 2 cups (300 g) chopped strawberries or raspberries, plus a few sliced for the top
- Sweetener to taste

---

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Line a rimmed baking sheet (about 10x15 in; the post does not give a pan size) with parchment.
2. Blend the cottage cheese, eggs, cornstarch and protein powder until completely smooth.
3. Spread the batter into a thin, even rectangle on the parchment. Bake 20-25 minutes, until lightly golden and set.
4. Let it cool slightly.
5. Mix the yogurt, cream cheese, sweetener and chopped berries.
6. Spread the filling over the base and top with a few sliced strawberries.
7. Roll into a log, using the parchment to help lift and curl it.
8. Chill until firm, then slice into 4.

---

## Notes

- Blend the base very smooth. If it cracks when rolling, roll while it is still slightly warm.
- Pan size and directions were inferred from the post; adjust bake time to your pan.
- Keep covered in the fridge, best within 3 days.
