## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup (113 g) cottage cheese
- 1 large egg
- 1/2 cup (56 g) shredded mozzarella cheese
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder (optional)
- 1 pinch salt (optional)

---

## Directions
1. Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Blend the cottage cheese until completely smooth and creamy.
3. Transfer to a bowl. Whisk in the egg, then stir in the mozzarella, garlic powder and salt.
4. Spoon onto the parchment and spread into an even rectangle about 1/4 inch (0.6 cm) thick.
5. Bake 15-18 minutes, until the top is golden and the center is firm. For crispier edges, broil 1-2 minutes, watching closely.
6. Cool on the pan 2-3 minutes, then cut into 6-8 breadsticks with a sharp knife or pizza cutter.
7. Serve warm.

---

## Tips
- Blend the cottage cheese until very smooth for the best texture.
- Keep the rectangle an even thickness so it cooks uniformly.
- Let the slab rest a couple of minutes before slicing so it firms up.
- Watch closely if broiling; the cheese browns fast.
