## Ingredients

- 5 oz (142 g) lean ground beef (93/7)
- 7 oz (200 g) potatoes, russet or yellow
- 1 oz (28 g) reduced-fat cheddar cheese, shredded (about 1/4 cup)
- 3 tbsp (45 g) plain Greek yogurt (0-2% fat)
- 1/2 cup (75 g) diced tomatoes
- 2 tbsp (20 g) diced red onion
- 1 tbsp (10 g) sliced jalapeño, optional
- 1 tbsp (7 g) taco seasoning
- 1 tsp (5 ml) olive oil
- Salt and pepper, for the potatoes
- Splash of water, for the beef

---

## Directions

1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Dice the potatoes, toss with the olive oil, salt and pepper, and roast 20 to 25 minutes, until crisp.
2. Cook the ground beef in a skillet over medium heat. Add the taco seasoning and a splash of water, and simmer 2 to 3 minutes.
3. Put the roasted potatoes and seasoned beef in a bowl.
4. Top with the cheddar, Greek yogurt, tomatoes, red onion and jalapeño (if using).
5. Serve.
