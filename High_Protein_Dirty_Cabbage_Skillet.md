# High Protein Dirty Cabbage Skillet

> Smoky one-pan ground beef and cabbage skillet with peppers. Big volume, high protein, low sat fat, easy to batch for the week.

## Ingredients

- 2 lb 95-96% lean ground beef
- 1 large green cabbage, chopped
- 1 green bell pepper, diced
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 2 tbsp diced yellow onion
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 cup chicken bone broth
- 2 tsp smoked paprika, divided
- 1/2 tsp salt, divided
- 1/2 tsp black pepper, divided
- Olive oil (about 1 tsp per serving, to finish)

---

## Directions

1. **Brown the beef** in a large skillet with half of the smoked paprika, salt and pepper.
2. **Build the base** — add the onion and garlic and sauté 2-3 minutes. Stir in the tomato paste and Worcestershire.
3. **Add the cabbage** — stir in the chopped cabbage, bone broth and the remaining seasonings. Cover and simmer 12-15 minutes.
4. **Finish** — stir in the bell peppers and cook uncovered 5-7 minutes, until the vegetables are tender.
5. **Serve** — divide into four portions and drizzle each with 1 tsp olive oil.
6. **Store** — refrigerate covered up to 4-5 days. Reheats well.

---

## Notes

- A large head of cabbage fills the pan at first but cooks down a lot. Brown the beef in a very large skillet or Dutch oven.
- Use low-sodium bone broth to keep sodium down. Most of the sodium is in the broth and the salt.
- Fiber is modest for the volume. To raise it, stir in a drained can of no-salt-added black or white beans (+3-4g fiber and +5g protein per serving).
- Add the olive oil per portion when serving. It adds about 40 kcal each.
