## Ingredients

- 523 g (about 2 cups, or 3.5 single 5.3 oz cups) vanilla Oikos Triple Zero Greek yogurt
- 1 oz (1 small box) banana sugar-free instant pudding mix
- 64 g (about 1/2 cup) Keebler Mini Graham Cracker Crust, crushed (the original used Kodiak Cookie Butter granola)

---

## Directions

1. Crush the graham crust into fine crumbs in a food processor or in a bag by hand.
2. Whisk the yogurt and pudding mix until very thick and smooth.
3. Fold most of the crumbs into the yogurt. Keep a little back for topping.
4. Divide between 3 cups and sprinkle the reserved crumbs on top.
5. Serve right away, or chill at least 1 hour for a thicker texture.

---

## Notes

- Using the granola instead of graham crust swaps some carbs for fat and a bit of extra fiber.
- Three servings keeps carbs near 28g each, a good fit after a lift.
- Best the same day; crumbs soften in the fridge.
